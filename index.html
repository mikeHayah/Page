01About

I develop machine learning models that learn structured representations of complex biological systems, the dynamics of molecular systems and capture the structure of chemical space.

I am passionate about self-supervised learning, cross-modal representation learning, generative modeling, and geometric deep learning. Three themes run through it:

  1. 01

    Generative models for molecules

    Property-guided, E(3)-equivariant diffusion that steers generation toward sparse, unexplored regions of chemical space.

    QALPA →
  2. 02

    Cross-model and self-supervised representations

    Learning joint predictive representations from cross-model data without labels, and keeping them stable and informative.

  3. 03

    Physics in the loop

    Coupling learning with quantum chemistry and mechanistic models, so that models are checked against — and improved by — physical evaluation.

    Projects →

Trained in biomedical engineering at Cairo University and computational life science at TU Dresden, I’ve worked across molecular generation, representation learning, bioimage analysis and simulation for surgical robotics.
